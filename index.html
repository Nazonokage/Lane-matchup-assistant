League of Legends & Wild Rift draft guidance

Lane Ledger is a free champion matchup assistant for League PC and Wild Rift. Browse all champions or filter by bot / Dragon, Support, Mid, Jungle and top / Baron lane. Use the Draft Helper to explore available counter-pick notes and bot duo synergies.

How do I find a missing champion?

Select the correct game, open the Almanac and choose All champions. Every listed champion has gameplay guidance, counterplay advice and a key cooldown or resource to watch. Role filters show the supported lane plans.

Are these live win rates?

No. Patch summaries link to Riot’s notes, while draft scores explain team composition and selected matchup interactions. Named matchup coverage is limited; an unlisted matchup is unknown, not neutral. PC and Wild Rift have separate rosters and references. Draft selections are not saved between visits.
